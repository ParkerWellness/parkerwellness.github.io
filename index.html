Hospital Hub (SugarLand Brain)

Hospital Hub is the all-in-one workspace for the MHSL Emergency Medicine team. Built for fast-paced clinical operations, it brings team communication, critical documents, contact directories, and daily workflow tools into one secure app.

With Hospital Hub, your team can quickly access protocols and guidelines, browse and manage shared documents, post updates, and find physicians, APPs, and consultant contacts when minutes matter.

The app also includes an AI-powered Q&A assistant that helps surface answers from your internal MHSL documents with source context, plus personal tools like notes and to-do tracking to stay organized during shifts.
