Hospital Hub (SugarLand Brain)

Hospital Hub is the all-in-one workspace for the MHSL Emergency Medicine team. Built for fast-paced clinical operations, it brings team communication, critical documents, contact directories, and daily workflow tools into one secure app.

With Hospital Hub, clinicians can quickly access protocols and guidelines, browse and manage shared documents, post updates, and find physicians, APPs, and consultant contacts when minutes matter.

The app includes an AI-powered Q&A assistant that surfaces answers from internal MHSL documents with source-backed context, along with personal tools such as notes and to-do tracking to help clinicians stay organized during shifts.

Key Features

  • Secure sign-in with role-based and administrative controls
  • Home-tile dashboard for rapid access to team resources
  • Shared document library with search and organization
  • Team notifications and communication tools
  • Physician, APP, and consultant directories
  • AI document Q&A with source-backed responses
  • Personal notes and to-do tools
  • Subscription support for premium access

Designed specifically for MHSL clinicians and staff, Hospital Hub helps keep teams aligned, informed, and ready to deliver care.
