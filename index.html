Privacy Policy

Last updated:

Parker Wellness, PLLC (“Parker Wellness,” “we,” “us”) develops Hospital Hub, a secure workspace for clinical operations. This Privacy Policy describes how we handle information when you use our products and services.

1. Information we may process

  • Account and authentication data: identifiers used for secure sign-in, role-based access, and account management.
  • App content and usage data: content you upload or create in the app (e.g., notes, documents, posts), and feature usage needed to operate the service.
  • Device and technical data: basic device identifiers, diagnostic information, and logs used to maintain performance and security.

2. How we use information

  • Provide, operate, and maintain the app’s core features (documents, directories, updates, notes, to-dos, and related tools).
  • Enable secure access controls (role-based/admin controls) and protect the integrity of team resources.
  • Improve reliability, diagnose issues, and support users.
  • Provide subscription functionality where enabled.

3. AI document Q&A

Hospital Hub may include an AI-powered assistant designed to surface answers from internal documents with source-backed context. The assistant is intended to support retrieval of internal operational knowledge.

  • AI features may process user-provided queries and relevant document excerpts to generate responses.
  • Availability and configuration may vary by deployment and organizational settings.

4. Sharing and access

Access to app content may be restricted to authorized users within an organization, based on roles and administrative controls. We do not sell personal information.

5. Security

We implement administrative controls and technical safeguards appropriate for software used in clinical environments. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure, but we work to protect confidentiality and integrity.

6. Data retention

Retention practices can vary depending on organizational settings and deployment configuration. We retain information as needed to provide the service and comply with applicable requirements.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, please use our Support page.

Do not submit sensitive patient information through support email or website contact channels.
